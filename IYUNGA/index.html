ABOUT THE SCHOOL

A school built on discipline and curiosity

Founded in 1978, Iyunga Secondary School sits on the slopes above Mbeya town, serving day and boarding students from Form I through Form VI. We combine the national O-Level and A-Level curriculum with strong co-curricular programmes in sport, debate and the sciences.

01

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality secondary education that equips every learner with knowledge, character and confidence.

02

Our Vision

To be a leading secondary school in the Southern Highlands, recognised for academic excellence and civic responsibility.

03

Our Values

Discipline, integrity, hard work and respect — the same values printed on every school badge since 1978.

ADMISSIONS

How to join Iyunga Secondary School

Form I selection follows the national PSLE placement system; Form V applications are made directly to the school.

  1. 1

    Check eligibility

    Form I: national selection list. Form V: O-Level certificate with required division.

  2. 2

    Collect the form

    Pick up an application form from the school office or download it below.

  3. 3

    Submit documents

    Return the form with certificates, a birth certificate and passport photos.

  4. 4

    Interview & confirmation

    Shortlisted candidates attend a short interview before a place is confirmed.
